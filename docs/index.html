PROJECT MEMORY FOR AI AGENTS

Stop juggling chat windows.
Complete your project in one seamless conversation.

Marvnor is a proprietary, hosted MCP/API service that gives your AI agent persistent project memory. Your agent saves confirmed or evidence-backed requirements, decisions, and changes. For each new question, Marvnor retrieves relevant saved records, verifies claims against them, and flags conflicting information for your confirmation. Records can be corrected or deleted as your project evolves.

Spend less time repeating the background and untangling old decisions. Pass only the current question and relevant results to your model to reduce repeated history in its input and keep your project moving.
